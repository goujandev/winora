Winora uses Velopack 1.2.0 for installation and updates.
Velopack: https://github.com/velopack/velopack (MIT licence).
Its copyright and licence notices accompany its upstream NuGet package.

Taskbar effects use the separate, unmodified TranslucentTB 2026.2 executable.
It is downloaded directly from the official TranslucentTB release on first use.
Winora's installer does not redistribute or link TranslucentTB code.
Project and source: https://github.com/TranslucentTB/TranslucentTB/tree/2026.2
Licence: https://github.com/TranslucentTB/TranslucentTB/blob/2026.2/LICENSE.md
TranslucentTB is distributed by its authors under GPLv3.
The engine retains its upstream name, signature, and independent tray menu.

Optional window tiling uses the separate, unmodified GlazeWM 3.10.1 executable.
It is downloaded directly from the official glzr-io/glazewm release on first use.
Winora's installer does not contain or link GlazeWM code. GlazeWM retains its
upstream identity and signature; Winora supplies its own configuration.
Project and corresponding source: https://github.com/glzr-io/glazewm/tree/v3.10.1
Licence: https://github.com/glzr-io/glazewm/blob/v3.10.1/LICENSE.md
GlazeWM is distributed by its authors under GPLv3.

Missing Microsoft UI and VC framework packages are downloaded directly from
Microsoft's official NuGet packages or microsoft/winget-cli GitHub releases.
They are installed as signed per-user Windows framework packages.
Microsoft's applicable licence terms apply to those packages.
